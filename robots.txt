{% comment %}
  The store-redirect endpoints (/get-nri-meet/, /get-koottali/, /ref/) are kept
  crawlable on purpose and carry a `noindex` meta tag instead. Disallowing them
  here would stop crawlers from ever reading that tag, which is what actually
  keeps them out of the index.

  The AI crawlers below are already covered by the `User-agent: *` rule above.
  They are listed explicitly anyway for two reasons: several of these operators
  treat an explicit named rule as the authoritative signal, and being listed by
  name means a future change to the wildcard default cannot quietly remove
  access. Each is a deliberate opt-in — we want NRI Meet cited in AI answers
  about Indian dating apps.
{% endcomment %}
